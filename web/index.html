What is Cashu & How Does This Work?
cashu.space ↗

Cashu is an open-source Chaumian Ecash protocol built on Bitcoin Lightning (learn more at cashu.space ↗). Normally, market making on Lightning requires running your own routing node or paying recurring channel rental fees whenever your BTC channel fills up on one side.

This vault connects to the community Cashu Mint to act as an automatic push/pull liquidity buffer for your Bitcoin channel:

  • MINT → (Restore Inbound): When your BTC channel fills up locally and runs low on Receive capacity, the bot sweeps sats over Lightning into Ecash bearer tokens so you can keep receiving BTC on trades.
  • ← MELT (Restore Outbound): When your BTC channel runs low on Send capacity, the bot melts those Ecash tokens back into your Lightning channel on demand.
⚠️ Important Risks to Understand
  • Custodial Mint Risk: While Chaumian blind signatures protect your privacy, the community Cashu Mint holds the backing Bitcoin while your sats sit in the vault. If the Mint goes permanently offline or fails, unmelted Ecash held in the vault cannot be recovered.
  • Local Bearer Token Storage: Your Ecash proofs are stored locally on your machine (data/cashu_wallet.json) like digital cash. Anyone with access to that file or exported cashuA... tokens can spend them, and deleting the file without a backup loses unmelted proofs.
  • Short-Term Rebalancing Buffer Only: Only use the Cashu Vault as a working liquidity buffer for active market making — not for long-term storage. You can melt your Ecash back into your channel or export cashuA... tokens at any time.
